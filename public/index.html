How quick-add works

Type in plain English, include a number — it figures out the rest and shows a preview before you hit Add.

  • An expense: "500 grocery", "1200 for mobile bill" — just an amount + what it was for.
  • You borrowed: "took 2000 from Ahmed", "borrowed 500 from Dad" — needs "from <name>".
  • You lent: "gave 1000 to Bilal", "lent 300 to Hassan" — needs "to <name>".

Rent isn't supported here since it needs a due date — add that from the Rent page.
